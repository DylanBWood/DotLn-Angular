Repair rule: code that only a lazy route uses stays out of the initial chunk.
Record the production initial total and its per-package attribution before
and after the repair, against the 495.40 kB base, and keep the declared
budgets unchanged. Make no change that loads the catalog or its filters less
efficiently just to meet the number. If the most efficient arrangement still
exceeds a declared threshold, record that measurement and its reason in a
decision and take criterion 7's wording to the operator rather than forcing
it.

The operator stated the standard during this verification. They support
failing on avoidable inefficiency, early. A budget overrun that is the most
efficient arrangement is not a failure, and a rule that forces one is
lose-lose. This finding rests on the measured, avoidable overrun, not on the
threshold alone.
