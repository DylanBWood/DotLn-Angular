### What the wall study's avoidance did that Seek alone did not

Read from the page's own frame descriptions (`defaultRun` and `oneUnitRun` in
the results). The nine-unit ray reaches the wall on frames 5 and 6 only, at 0.5
and 0.6 s. For those two frames avoidance replaces the delivery point with a
point six units back out from the wall along its normal, (4.00, 4.15) and then
(4.00, 5.18), so the courier swings further towards the wall's top end than
seeking alone would: its heading is 36.0° against 29.5° at 0.7 s, the largest
gap, about 6.5°. From frame 7 it seeks the delivery again, and the two headings
converge on about 43° by 2 s. That early nudge is all avoidance does. At 2.9 s,
rounding the wall's top end, the courier clears it by 0.12 units; in the same
run with the one-unit ray, which never hits, Seek alone carries the body 0.08
units into the wall's end on two frames. Seeking the offset delivery does the
turning; avoidance turns a slight overlap into a narrow miss. A ray of 10 units
or more hits from the first frame and does worse, 0.74 units of overlap at
3.9 s, which the limits state.

The page says this too: its caption calls the effect "a small clearance
correction, not sustained steering along the wall". The comparison chart is the
one place the reader cannot see it: on its −0.08 to 12.19 axis the 0.2-unit
difference is under 2% of the height, so the two traces read as one line, and
the findings below the chart carry the difference (F3).

## Review of the implementation

The reshape does what the Objective describes, and it reads as an example worth
copying. D060's worst case, Two-level formation steering's `escortWorkedLines`,
took twelve positional parameters with inline object types and a
`ReturnType<typeof calculateEscortStep>`; it now takes the translator and one
`EscortFrame`. Each module describes its run and its frame once, in interfaces
beside the functions that read them, and binds its number format once through
`studies/number-format.ts`, whose four options (precision, near-zero threshold,
negative-zero normalisation, trailing-zero trim) keep each study's authored
rounding; the 39 unchanged studies' identical output across 302 input cases is
the evidence that no rounding moved. The corpus guard's brackets are simple and
strict: letters outside a resolved message fail, a literal parameter fails
unless it is one of three declared machine values, and its own unit cases pin
the edge forms (nested messages, scientific notation, a word offered as a marble
symbol). The drawing's three repairs are small and legible: a guide now ends
where the line from its anchor meets the name's box, a vector too short for a
head is not drawn, and label room scales with the font size the media query
selects, with the listener removed on destroy. The marble helper's explicit
`descriptions` mode replaces a translator-identity switch, and only combineLatest
